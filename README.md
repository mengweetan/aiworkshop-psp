# Sagelion AI Workshop: Build Your Agent
## Presentation Materials Package

This package contains all materials needed to deliver the 3-hour "Build Your Agent" workshop for pMET professionals, teachers, and young retirees.

---

## 📁 Files Included

### 1. **Sagelion_AI_Workshop_Presentation.md**
The complete slide deck in Markdown format.
- **Use for:** Main presentation delivery
- **Format:** Ready to convert to slides (PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote)
- **Duration:** ~3 hours with breaks
- **Sections:** 
  - Part A: Foundations (50 min)
  - Part B: Tools by Audience (60 min)
  - Part C: Engagement & Action (35 min)

### 2. **AI_Stack_Cheat_Sheet.md**
One-page handout for attendees.
- **Use for:** Print and distribute to all attendees
- **Contains:** Quick reference, decision tree, cost comparison, next steps
- **Print format:** Single sheet, double-sided recommended

### 3. **Speaker_Notes_and_Tips.md**
Comprehensive facilitator guide.
- **Use for:** Presenter preparation and delivery guidance
- **Contains:** 
  - Delivery tips for each section
  - Response scripts for common questions
  - Timing management strategies
  - Troubleshooting scenarios
  - Post-workshop follow-up checklist

### 4. **Hands_On_Station_Guide.md**
Detailed instructions for the practical session.
- **Use for:** Station facilitators during hands-on segment
- **Contains:**
  - Setup requirements for each of 3 stations
  - Step-by-step attendee instructions
  - Expected outputs and variations
  - Troubleshooting tips
  - Conversion signal indicators

### 5. **README.md** (this file)
Package overview and usage guide.

| Segment | Size | Pain Points | Workshop Goal |
|---------|------|-------------|---------------|

## 🛠️ Technical Requirements

### Main Presentation
- **Display:** Projector or large screen (HDMI connection)
- **Sound:** Mic + speakers (venue-dependent)
- **Internet:** Stable WiFi for live demos (have backup plan)
- **Laptop:** Presenter's device with slides and backup USB

- Task cards printed

**Station C: Retirees**

- Task cards printed

## 📊 Success Metrics

### During Workshop
- **Engagement:** Questions asked, hands-on participation
- **Comprehension:** Successful task completion rate at stations

### Post Workshop
- **Immediate (day-of):** Booking rate (target: 30%+)

- **2 weeks:** Standard tier inquiries

### Conversion Benchmarks
- **Excellent:** 30%+ booking rate
- **Good:** 20-30%

## 💡 Key Messages (Memorize These)

### For pMET Professionals
> "You didn't get promoted to spend 40% of your week on status updates and email triage."

### For Teachers
> "You trained to teach children, not to chase parents for consent forms."

### For Retirees
> "The AI revolution isn't a youth club. The biggest users in Japan are over 60."

> "AI replaces tasks, not roles. The question isn't 'Will AI take my job?' but 'Will someone using AI take my job?'"

### On Cost
> "Pilot: SGD 260 first year. That's less than one nice dinner per month to reclaim 5 hours per week."

### On Mistakes
> "AI will make mistakes. That's why we design for it: AI proposes, humans approve. Always."
