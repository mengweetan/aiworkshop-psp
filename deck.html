03 · Risk & deployment 15 / 26
// Three places the agent can live

Cloud. On-prem.
Hybrid.

/ 01 · Cloud SaaS

Fast.

Hours to days to set up. Lower cost. But data leaves your control.

Best for low-sensitivity pilots.

/ 02 · On-premise

Sovereign.

Full data control. PDPA-friendly. Customisable. Higher setup; needs IT.

Best for sensitive data.

/ 03 · Hybrid

Both.

Data on-prem. Models in cloud. Balance of control and convenience.

Best for mixed workloads.

03 · Risk & deployment 16 / 26
// PSP-specific

Four zones.
Four rules.

  • Public
    Event details, general announcements. Any AI model is fine.
  • Internal
    Member lists, attendance records. Private deployment preferred.
  • Confidential
    Financial data, personal member info. On-premise required.
  • Restricted
    Governance, personnel matters. Human-only. AI-assisted summaries at most.

06 · Hands-on 25 / 26
// Sixty minutes. Three or four groups.

Bring a workflow.
Leave with a working one.

  1. / 01
    Pick a workflow.
    One real PSP friction point. Map it on a page: current state · pain points · agentic opportunity.
  2. / 02
    Text the agent.
    Plain language via WhatsApp or Telegram. No coding. Just describe what you need done.
  3. / 03
    Watch it work.
    Reads the demo calendar, mailbox, files. Audit log open. Nothing hidden.
  4. / 04
    Iterate the prompt.
    More formal. Add a deadline. Tighten the tone. The work changes faster than a paragraph.
  5. / 05
    Present back.
    Five minutes. What worked. What surprised you. What you'd actually deploy on Monday.
