02
The shift

A chatbot answers. An agent acts.

Most of what was called "AI" until last year was a polite text generator on the end of a chat box. The agentic shift is small in the prompt and large in the consequence — agents read your files, hold context across sessions, and execute multi-step tasks until done.

Then · 2022–2024

The chatbot.

  • Responds to prompts. Forgets you between sessions.
  • Text in, text out. No file system, no tools.
  • One conversation at a time. No schedules. No callbacks.
  • Cannot take action — it can only describe one.
Now · 2025–2026

The agent.

  • Proactively monitors. Acts on your behalf, within rules you set.
  • Reads email, calendars, files, APIs, databases.
  • Runs scheduled and parallel workflows. Wakes itself.
  • Logs every action. Asks before doing the irreversible.
Three pillars worth holding onto
✦

Governance first.

Data sovereignty, transparency, accountability, PDPA compliance, human oversight — settled before the agent is plugged in, not after.

◌

Local-first by default.

Sensitive data stays on your infrastructure. Models are hot-swappable. The agent runs where you do, not where the vendor wants you to.

◇

Human in the loop.

AI proposes. Humans approve high-stakes actions. Every step is logged, reviewable, and reversible — never a black box.

04
Hands-on · 60 minutes

Bring a workflow. Leave with a working one.

The last hour belongs to you. Three or four groups, one PSP workflow each, a facilitator at the elbow, and a live agent on the table. We end with a five-minute presentation per group — not a slide, an actual result.

  1. Pick a workflow.
    Choose one real PSP friction point. Map it on a single page: current state · pain points · the agentic opportunity.
  2. Text the agent.
    Send a plain-language request via WhatsApp or Telegram. No coding, no console — just describe what you need done.
  3. Watch it work.
    The agent accesses the demo calendar, mailbox, and files. Read the audit log line-by-line; nothing it does is hidden.
  4. Iterate the prompt.
    Make it more formal. Add a deadline reminder. Tighten the tone. The shape of the work changes faster than a paragraph in a document.
  5. Present back.
    Five minutes to your group. What worked, what surprised you, what you would actually deploy on Monday.
